# v0.14.1 population and resource usability patch

This corrective release addresses two issues identified during live platform testing: stale year-wise population projections and an overly workforce-oriented presentation of Resource & Capacity Planning.

## Reactive population projections

The previous Population & Uptake page calculated a fresh annual projection from the current population drivers, but then displayed year-specific Streamlit number inputs with persistent widget keys. Once those widgets had been created, changing population growth, covered lives or funnel inputs could leave the visible year-wise values at their earlier values.

v0.14.1 separates two behaviours explicitly:

- **Use formula-derived annual values** — annual values are calculated directly from the current drivers on every rerun. Changing a driver changes the year-wise population immediately.
- **Edit annual values manually** — year-specific values are independent overrides. The formula projection remains visible for comparison, and a reset button copies the current formula values into the manual fields.

The software no longer presents stale manual widget values as though they were the current formula projection.

## Clearer population semantics

Direct population mode now distinguishes:

- Year-1 eligible population;
- eligible-population growth;
- optional covered lives;
- covered-lives growth.

Covered lives are an optional denominator for outputs such as PMPM and do not alter the eligible population in direct mode.

Top-down mode makes the population funnel explicit:

`eligible population = covered/catchment population × prevalence × diagnosed/identified × clinically eligible × access/coverage`

Growth in top-down mode applies to the covered/catchment population; the eligible population then changes through the same funnel assumptions.

Growth inputs are displayed as percentages rather than decimal fractions.

## Resource & Capacity Planning is unit-agnostic

The capacity engine was already based on arbitrary natural units, but the original default and wording made the interface appear centred on staff hours. v0.14.1 makes the generality explicit.

Common examples now include:

- clinical staff hours;
- treatment-chair hours;
- inpatient bed-days;
- syringes;
- medicine vials;
- ambulance trips;
- vehicle-hours;
- oxygen supply;
- blood products;
- diagnostic scans.

Users may still create any custom physical resource and unit.

## Unit coherence safeguard

Demand and capacity must use the same natural unit. For example, if inpatient demand is expressed in bed-days, available capacity should also be expressed in bed-days rather than compared directly with a count of physical beds. Any conversion from a stock (such as staffed beds) to an annual flow capacity should be explicit and documented.

The platform does not silently convert money into physical resources, or one physical unit into another.
